About the property

Description property

No. 3 Bay View Cottage is an end of terrace 3 bedroom, 2 bathroom, 950 sq. ft. property with sea view built in 2000 and centrally located in the charming coastal village of Kilcrohane on the Sheep's Head Peninsula in south west Cork. Option to purchase contents.

LOCATION:

The property is conveniently situated only a stone's throw from a shop, post office, bar and 2 restaurants. It is a short stroll to the sea, a children's playground with tennis court, a community playing field and a pier. Bantry, the closest town, is a 30 minute journey by car and Cork city and airport are less than 2 hours by car. There are 5 daily round trip buses from the village to and from Bantry and beyond.
